## What I worked on

- Basic image preprocessing with OpenCV.
- Transfer learning with MobileNetV2.
- Four-class vehicle classification: bus, car, motor and truck.
- YOLO11n vehicle detection.
- ByteTrack object tracking.
- Tracking car, motorcycle, bus and truck classes across frames.
- Producing annotated video frames with bounding boxes, confidence and tracking IDs.
